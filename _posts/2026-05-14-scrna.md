<b><u>Project:</u> Compare Sequencing Platforms in Terms of Immune Repertoire Analysis</b>

<b><u>Sequencing Platforms:</u> Illumina NextSeq short read vs PacBio+Kinnex long read</b>

<b><u>Assumptions:</u></b>

Matched sample pairs were analyzed using short-read and long-read single-cell sequencing workflows generated from the same 10x Genomics cDNA libraries (verify the fluorescence intensity and molarity distribution with Bioanalyser). Sequencing libraries passed standard quality control metrics (e.g., PacBio, Table2 [1](#ref1); Illumina, Table3 [2](#ref2)) and that comparable analytical tools and workflows were applied for both datasets.


> Evaluation of immune receptor recovery across cell types [3](#ref3)

- Compare immune cell-type detection between sequencing methods by assessing whether specific populations (e.g., γδ T-cells) are differentially recovered. (<i>PacBio/Kinnex passes if it recovers the same major immune populations without systematic loss of rare cell populations.</i>)

- Assess full-length paired-chain recovery within detected B-cell (e.g., H/L chains) and T-cell populations (e.g., α/β chains) across clonotypes to evaluate immune receptor reconstruction. (<i>PacBio/Kinnex passes if it achieves higher full-length paired-chain recovery across individual single-cell barcodes.</i>)

> Evaluation of platform-dependent differences in recombination profiles and clonal architecture [4](#ref4)

- Comparison of V(D)J gene usage and recombination patterns across both BCR and TCR chains, including IGHV vs IGHD, IGHV vs IGHJ, IGKV vs IGKJ, and TRAV vs TRAJ gene combinations. Clonal architecture can also be evaluated using diversity metrics (e.g., Shannon diversity) for each immunoglobulin and T cell receptor chain. (<i>PacBio/Kinnex passes if it yields a denser V(D)J matrix with higher read-count abundance and fewer dropouts (e.g., visualization with heatmap), and if it yields overall higher diversity.</i>)

- Method-specific and shared recombination patterns can also be summarized. CDR3 structure comparisons can include complete CDR3 recovery rate, CDR3 length distribution and CDR3 motif structure analysis between sequencing methods.(<i>PacBio/Kinnex passes if it shows high proportion of CDR3 recovery rate, unbiased CDR3 length distribution (need more detailed inspection for actual CDR3 length), comparable CDR3 motifs while also capturing hidden sequence variants.</i>)
